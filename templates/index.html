About GreenTrack

GreenTrack is a web-based community recycling tracking and ranking system developed to support sustainable waste management initiatives in Sibu. It provides a centralized platform for residents, recycling merchants, and municipal officers to manage recycling activities in a transparent and structured manner.

♻️ Tracking Records recycling activity clearly.
🏆 Ranking Encourages participation through leaderboard.
📊 Reporting Supports organized data monitoring.

System Objectives

🔐 Secure Role-Based Web Platform

To design and implement a secure, role-based web platform that allows residents, recycling merchants, and municipal council officers to register, log in, and interact with the system.

🏆 Gamified Engagement & Recognition

To integrate a gamified engagement module featuring a recycling participation leaderboard and an annual recognition award system.

🧪 System Evaluation & User Acceptance

To evaluate the system’s functionality, usability, and reliability through structured testing and user acceptance feedback.

📊 Data Integrity & Reporting Support

To ensure data integrity and accessibility through consistent data entry, update, and reporting mechanisms.
