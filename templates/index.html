About GreenTrack

GreenTrack is a web-based community recycling tracking and ranking system developed to support sustainable waste management initiatives in Sibu. It provides a centralized platform for residents, recycling merchants, and municipal officers to manage recycling activities in a transparent and structured manner.

♻️ Tracking Records recycling activity clearly.
🏆 Ranking Encourages participation through leaderboard.
📊 Reporting Supports organized data monitoring.

📢 Upcoming Recycling Events Stay updated with community activities
